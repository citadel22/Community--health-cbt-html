# Community Health CBT Mock Exams

Welcome to the Community Health Computer-Based Testing (CBT) Mock Exams platform. This website provides comprehensive mock examinations to help you prepare for your community health assessments.

## 📋 Features

- **Multiple Exam Levels**: Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced difficulty levels
- **Realistic Testing Experience**: Simulates actual computer-based testing environment
- **Timed Exams**: Each exam has a time limit to build speed and accuracy
- **Immediate Feedback**: Get detailed explanations for each answer after submission
- **Score Tracking**: See your performance metrics and areas for improvement
- **Comprehensive Topics**: Coverage of all major community health areas

1. **Mock Exam 1: Fundamentals of Community Health**

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Visit the website: **https://citadel22.github.io/Community--health-cbt-html/**
2. Browse the available exams
3. Click "Start Exam" on your chosen exam
4. Answer all questions within the time limit
5. Submit to see your results and detailed feedback

## 📊 How to Use

### Taking an Exam
- Read each question carefully
- Select your answer from the provided options
- Use the timer to manage your time effectively
- Review your answers before submitting

### Understanding Results
- Your score is displayed as a percentage
- Passing score is 70% or above
- Each question includes an explanation of the correct answer
- Use feedback to identify areas for improvement

## 🎓 Study Tips

- Take exams in quiet environments for better focus
- Start with beginner-level exams and progress to advanced ones
- Review explanations for incorrect answers
- Take notes on topics you find challenging
- Retake exams to improve your score
- Study relevant materials between attempts
